Elevance Legal Services Brand Identity

Fused "E + L" Monogram Concepts

Exploring sophisticated Didone / Modern serif fusions of the letters E (Elevance) and L (Legal), maintaining the exact weight, geometry, and squircle silhouette of the original mark.

Option 01 • Recommended

01. The Horizon Baseline

The vertical stem and top/middle arms preserve the exact Didone serif anatomy of the original "E", while the lower arm sweeps forward uninterrupted into an extended "L" foundation with a sharp upright chisel terminal.

  • • Identical visual weight and counter proportions to original image
  • • Subtly reads as "E" at first glance and reveals the unified "L" foot
  • • Symbolizes a solid legal foundation and elevated forward trajectory
