01—Your goals

Where are you now?

Every situation goes through distinct stages. Knowing what to do next is often the hardest part. Pick the stage you are at.

Your home

Housing and landlord–tenant matters, handled step by step with organised paperwork and clear communication.

  1. 01 Understand paperwork Tenancy documentation and correspondence: what it says and what information is needed.
  2. 02 Raise the issue Disrepair concerns, deposit queries and communications with your local authority, clearly written.
  3. 03 Organise evidence A clear, chronological record of who said what and when, ready for formal review.
  4. 04 When it goes further Where formal court proceedings or reserved legal work are required, referral to Ranjay Solicitors.

Your work

Employment and workplace matters, documented with a precise timeline and professional clarity.

  1. 01 Workplace documents Contracts, letters and employer policies: what they say and what they mean for you.
  2. 02 Prepare a grievance Well-structured grievance or complaint correspondence setting out the facts clearly.
  3. 03 Disciplinary evidence Presenting an unshakeable chronological timeline of events, emails, and notes.
  4. 04 Solicitor referral Employment litigation and reserved legal activities are referred to Ranjay Solicitors.

Family & future

Sensitive paperwork prepared with dignity: family records, wills, and lasting powers of attorney.

  1. 01 Family matters Gathering records, preparing correspondence and coordinating next steps.
  2. 02 Wills & estate planning Gathering instructions and preparing straightforward wills and estate wishes.
  3. 03 Lasting Powers of Attorney Forms, administrative checks and registration for financial affairs or health.
  4. 04 Special Powers of Attorney Powers for specific transactions or overseas use, with notarisation assistance.

Your business

Routine commercial paperwork, terms, and contract management for London small businesses and entrepreneurs.

  1. 01 Commercial contracts Drafting terms of business, supply agreements, NDAs and contractor terms.
  2. 02 Contract admin Organising agreements, renewal dates, and variation records.
  3. 03 Dispute tracking When commercial counterparties fail to perform, preparing chronology.
  4. 04 Review readiness Bundling business records for external investors or formal audits.
  5. 05 Legal action High court litigation and reserved work via Ranjay Solicitors.

Languages & Triage

Professional translation and initial matter organization for Arabic and Kurdish speakers across the UK.

  1. 01 Arabic & Kurdish Official legal and commercial translation between Arabic, Kurdish and English.
  2. 02 Certified translation Birth certificates, marriage documents, powers of attorney and academic records.
  3. 03 Immigration triage Administrative bundling of papers and evidence before submission to solicitors.
  4. 04 Solicitor referral Regulated immigration or criminal defence representation via Ranjay Solicitors.

04—Regulatory compliance

Consultancy freedom,
solicitor authority.

Elevance Legal Services Ltd is an independent legal consultancy. We are not a law firm of solicitors and are not regulated by the Solicitors Regulation Authority (SRA).

Where your situation involves reserved legal activities (such as formal court litigation, conveyancing or reserved probate), we work alongside Ranjay Solicitors, an independent SRA-regulated firm. Any regulated services are subject to Ranjay Solicitors’ independent client care and terms.

Regulatory Notice

  • • Elevance provides unreserved legal consultancy, document drafting, case support, and translation.
  • • Ranjay Solicitors handles reserved legal activities and litigation independently.
  • • No advice on this website creates a solicitor-client relationship until formally engaged.

05—Questions

Frequently asked.

Everything you need to know about how we work, who does what, and how pricing works.

Are you a law firm? +

No. Elevance Legal Services Ltd is an independent legal consultancy. We handle document preparation, case coordination, wills, powers of attorney, and translation. For reserved legal activities such as court litigation, we refer clients to Ranjay Solicitors.

How does pricing work? +

We operate primarily on transparent fixed fees agreed upfront before any work commences. There are no running hourly clocks or hidden administrative add-ons.

Are your translations certified for official UK use? +

Yes. Our translations between Arabic, Kurdish and English are supplied with a signed certification statement detailing translator credentials, date, and verification suitable for UK government bodies, courts, and educational institutions.

How does the relationship with Ranjay Solicitors work? +

Where your matter requires reserved legal activities such as formal proceedings in court, conveyancing, or regulated immigration appeals, we introduce you directly to Ranjay Solicitors, an independent law firm authorised and regulated by the SRA.
